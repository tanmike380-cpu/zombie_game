# Zombie Units

## 1. Walker
Role: basic cannon-fodder zombie.

- Movement speed: slow
- HP: low
- Damage: low
- Main threat: numbers
- Hearing sensitivity: normal

## 2. Runner
Role: fast breakthrough unit.

- Movement speed: high; can outrun most infantry
- HP: low to medium
- Damage: medium
- Purpose: quickly close on ranged units and amplify local chaos
- Hearing sensitivity: high

## 3. Brute
Role: high-HP, high-damage frontline zombie.

- Movement speed: medium
- HP: high
- Damage: high
- Characteristic: can kill ordinary soldiers quickly and pressure frontline tanks
- Hearing sensitivity: normal

## 4. Zombie Hound
Role: high-mobility backline disruptor.

- Movement speed: very high
- HP: low
- Damage: medium
- Special ability: can vault selected low-level walls/obstacles; exact rules to be prototyped
- Hearing sensitivity: very high

## 5. Spitter
Role: ranged harassment that forces formation changes.

- Movement speed: medium
- HP: low to medium
- Damage: area or damage-over-time
- Special ability: spits toxic fluid
- Hearing sensitivity: normal

## 6. Giant
Role: siege/breach unit.

- Movement speed: slow
- HP: very high
- Damage: very high
- Extra threat to buildings and walls
- Hearing sensitivity: low to normal

## 7. Boss Zombie
Role: stage-specific boss encounter.

- Exact mechanics will be designed separately.
- Principle: a boss should change battlefield rules rather than simply having more HP.

## Noise Targeting Rule

- If a zombie directly detects a player unit/building, direct combat targeting has priority.
- If it has no direct target, it can move toward active Noise sources.
- Noise should guide roaming zombies toward where the player is fighting, not toward corpse locations.

## V1 Principles

- Walkers create numerical pressure.
- Runners break through firepower zones.
- Brutes pressure the frontline.
- Zombie Hounds enter quickly and react strongly to distant combat noise.
- Spitters force players to reposition formations.
- Giants break fortifications.
- Bosses create special-stage challenges.

# 僵尸兵种

## 1. 普通僵尸 Walker
定位：最基础炮灰。

- 移速：慢
- HP：低
- 伤害：低
- 威胁来源：数量
- 听觉敏感度：普通

## 2. 狂暴僵尸 Runner
定位：快速突破。

- 移速：高，可快于大部分步兵
- HP：低~中
- 伤害：中
- 用途：快速贴近远程单位、扩大局部混乱
- 听觉敏感度：高

## 3. 胖尸 Brute
定位：高血量、高伤害前排。

- 移速：中
- HP：高
- 伤害：高
- 特征：可以快速击杀普通士兵，对前排形成压力
- 听觉敏感度：普通

## 4. 尸犬 Zombie Hound
定位：高机动扰后。

- 移速：非常高
- HP：低
- 伤害：中
- 特殊能力：可翻越部分低等级墙体/障碍（具体规则待原型）
- 听觉敏感度：非常高

## 5. 毒液僵尸 Spitter
定位：远程骚扰 / 迫使玩家调整阵型。

- 移速：中
- HP：低~中
- 伤害：范围或持续伤害
- 特殊能力：喷射毒液
- 听觉敏感度：普通

## 6. 巨型僵尸 Giant
定位：攻坚单位。

- 移速：慢
- HP：非常高
- 伤害：非常高
- 对建筑/城墙有额外威胁
- 听觉敏感度：低~普通

## 7. Boss Zombie
定位：阶段性 Boss。

- 具体机制后续单独设计。
- 原则：Boss 应改变战场规则，而不是单纯增加 HP。

## Noise 目标规则

- 僵尸如果直接发现玩家单位/建筑，优先直接追击和攻击。
- 没有直接目标时，才根据当前有效的 Noise 声音源移动。
- Noise 的作用是把游荡僵尸引向玩家正在战斗的位置，而不是引向尸体位置。

## 第一版原则

- 普通尸负责数量压力。
- 狂暴尸负责突破火力网。
- 胖尸负责打前排。
- 尸犬负责快速切入，并且对远处战斗声音更敏感。
- 毒液尸负责逼迫阵型移动。
- 巨型尸负责攻城。
- Boss 负责特殊阶段挑战。
