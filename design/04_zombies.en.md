# Zombie Units

**Language / 语言:** [中文](04_zombies.md) | **English**

## 1. Walker
Role: basic cannon-fodder zombie.

- Movement speed: very slow
- HP: low
- Damage: low
- Main threat: numbers
- Blood Scent sensitivity: low

## 2. Runner
Role: fast breakthrough unit.

- Movement speed: high; can outrun most infantry
- HP: low to medium
- Damage: medium
- Purpose: quickly close on ranged units and amplify local chaos
- Blood Scent sensitivity: medium to high

## 3. Brute
Role: high-HP, high-damage frontline zombie.

- Movement speed: medium
- HP: high
- Damage: high
- Characteristic: can kill ordinary soldiers quickly and pressure frontline tanks
- Blood Scent sensitivity: medium

## 4. Zombie Hound
Role: high-mobility backline disruptor.

- Movement speed: very high
- HP: low
- Damage: medium
- Special ability: can vault selected low-level walls/obstacles; exact rules to be prototyped
- Blood Scent sensitivity: very high

## 5. Spitter
Role: ranged harassment that forces formation changes.

- Movement speed: medium
- HP: low to medium
- Damage: area or damage-over-time
- Special ability: spits toxic fluid
- Blood Scent sensitivity: medium

## 6. Giant
Role: siege/breach unit.

- Movement speed: slow
- HP: very high
- Damage: very high
- Extra threat to buildings and walls
- Blood Scent sensitivity: low to medium

## 7. Boss Zombie
Role: stage-specific boss encounter.

- Exact mechanics will be designed separately.
- Principle: a boss should change battlefield rules rather than simply having more HP.

## V1 Principles

- Walkers create numerical pressure.
- Runners break through firepower zones.
- Brutes pressure the frontline.
- Zombie Hounds enter quickly and create internal disruption.
- Spitters force players to reposition formations.
- Giants break fortifications.
- Bosses create special-stage challenges.
